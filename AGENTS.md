# Repository instructions

For company research requests, follow `finance/companies/README.md` for research scope, data definitions, page generation, and publication. The user's standing preference is to publish completed company research in the site's company analysis section and verify the live page. Preserve unrelated repository changes.

For scientific paper research and literature-report requests, follow `reports/paper-research/README.md`. The user has approved detailed problem modeling, step-by-step method transformations with equations, experimental conditions, original result figures and evidence-based comparisons as the standard. Reuse the site's shared branding and readable report typography; do not redesign the site for each article. Use `scripts/render-paper-report.py` and `assets/paper-report.css` for new reports. Preserve the accepted Pilot-Based reference article. Unless the current request limits the task to discussion, a draft or local-only work, the user's confirmed report workflow includes categorization, publication to this GitHub website, and verification of the matching deployment and live content. ISAC papers belong under Communication → ISAC (`communication/isac/`); route other topics by their research subject.
