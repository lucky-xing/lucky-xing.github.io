# Repository instructions

For industry and sector research, apply the industry research framework and include a dedicated **sector valuation and historical percentile** section. Define the sector universe, select a representative index, and explain any exposure mismatch between the index, ETF, and researched companies. Choose metrics appropriate to the business model and cycle, such as recurring PE and FCF yield for profitable equipment makers, PB/ROE for banks, normalized EV/EBITDA for cyclicals, and rNPV for pre-profit drug developers. Report the sector aggregate and methodology, including weighting, loss-making constituents, one-off gains, and index rebalances where relevant. Keep ETF prices and premium/discount separate from underlying sector valuation.

Use comparable historical data to plot 10-, 5-, and 3-year windows when supported. For shorter series, plot the entire available history and report only supported windows. Label the series, metric, dates, frequency, source, coverage, and current value. Compute current percentiles separately within each window, disclose percentile direction and sample count, and show relevant historical reference levels. Never infer a percentile from a price chart or splice incompatible valuation definitions. If data are incomplete, state the verified coverage and missing windows. Interpret valuation alongside normalized earnings, growth, margins, and cash flow. Use historical levels and earnings scenarios to set conditional entry, add, reduction, and invalidation rules for sector exposure. Translate those rules to stock and ETF price plans only when the mapping is supported, and report cost-adjusted reward/risk.

For company research requests, follow `finance/companies/README.md` for research scope, data definitions, page generation, and publication. The user's standing preference is to publish completed company research in the site's company analysis section and verify the live page. Preserve unrelated repository changes.

For scientific paper research and literature-report requests, follow `reports/paper-research/README.md`. The user has approved detailed problem modeling, step-by-step method transformations with equations, experimental conditions, original result figures and evidence-based comparisons as the standard. Reuse the site's shared branding and readable report typography; do not redesign the site for each article. Use `scripts/render-paper-report.py` and `assets/paper-report.css` for new reports. Preserve the accepted Pilot-Based reference article. Unless the current request limits the task to discussion, a draft or local-only work, the user's confirmed report workflow includes categorization, publication to this GitHub website, and verification of the matching deployment and live content. ISAC papers belong under Communication → ISAC (`communication/isac/`); route other topics by their research subject.
